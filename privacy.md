# Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

Monthly Channel Report is a personal application used by the application
owner to create Excel reports from YouTube channel and YouTube Analytics data.

## Data accessed

The application accesses YouTube channel information, video information,
and YouTube Analytics report data authorized by the user.

## Use of data

The accessed data is used only to generate Excel reports requested by the user.

## Storage

OAuth credentials are stored securely for the purpose of maintaining the
authorized connection. Generated reports are stored only in locations selected
by the user.

## Data sharing

Google user data is not sold, transferred, or shared with third parties.

## Revoking access

The user can revoke the application's access at any time from the Google
Account third-party connections page:

https://myaccount.google.com/connections

## Contact

Questions about this application can be sent to the support email displayed
on the Google OAuth consent screen.
